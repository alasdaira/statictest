The world behind the story

Real lives. Theatrical legends.

Victorian London was a city of spectacle: music halls, crowded playhouses, celebrated performers and audiences hungry for wonder. Behind each glowing façade lay another city — of lodging houses, late-night cab rides and lives made precarious by applause.

The novel draws inspiration from this vivid world, including genuine figures such as Dan Leno, the beloved music-hall comedian, alongside fictional characters and imagined events.
